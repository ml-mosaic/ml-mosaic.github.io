01Meet v1

A sprite is a start, but games move. Characters walk, fires flicker, rain falls, coins spin. Animating pixel art by hand is even slower than drawing it: every frame is its own little drawing. v1 is our first try at doing the first draft of that for you. Describe a scene, and v1 will make a short, looping-friendly clip of it, in the same crisp style as m1.

16:9widescreen scenes, not just single sprites
12 fpsclassic pixel-art frame rate
2 sec24 hand-countable frames per clip
loopsseamless when you ask for one

v1 is a preview in the truest sense: it hasn't finished learning, so everything you see on this page is the kind of scene it's being taught to make, not something it has made. We'll show you its real output as soon as it's worth showing, gaps and all.

02What it's learning

Every clip v1 learns from is a little scene with a subject, an action, a place and a time of day, so you'll be able to ask for exactly that:

  • Characters on the move: walk cycles, runs and jumps, sometimes with a pet trotting behind.
  • Creatures: bouncing slimes, floating ghosts, flapping bats and birds, gliding dragons.
  • Places with a pulse: torch-lit dungeons, crackling campfires, rolling seas, turning windmills, sweeping lighthouses.
  • Weather and sky: rain, thunderstorms, snow, autumn leaves, cherry petals, fireflies, shooting stars and auroras.
  • Game feel: pickups that spin, float and sparkle, ready to drop into a menu or a level.

You'll be able to ask in whatever way feels natural, from "a knight walking to the right across a snowy field" to "can you animate a bat flying across the sky at night?".

03Made with care

Like m1, v1 learns only from our own dataset. No scraped artwork, no scraped video.

And when you ask for a seamless loop, we want it to really loop, the last frame flowing straight into the first, so it can sit in a game forever without a hitch.

Pixel art that moves, without the frame-by-frame grind.

04Where it stands

v1 is still training, and it's now in preview: on the Atelier plan it's in the Studio's model menu next to m1, and in the API, with 50 clips a day. A preview means exactly that: it's learning, it has gaps, and it gets better with every checkpoint. We'll keep showing its real output on the front page as it goes.

Not on Atelier? m1 is ready for everyone: make sprites in the Studio or through the API, and tell us which scenes you'd want v1 to animate first.
