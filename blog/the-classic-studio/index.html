01Three buttons

Until now the Studio asked you to pick a model before you could draw anything, and the list kept getting longer. So we've taken the question away. The classic Studio now has three buttons:

  • Image: sprites, from a sentence.
  • Animation: sprites that move.
  • Video: little pixel-art scenes (on Atelier).

Pick one, type what you want, and press Create. That's all.

02Picking the best model

Behind each button, the Studio chooses the best model that is switched on and included in your plan. Right now that's m2 for Free and m1+ for Artisan and Atelier (see m2 and m1+). When a better model arrives, your button starts using it, and you don't have to do anything. A small line next to the buttons always tells you which model it picked.

A button that has nothing for you yet says why: SOON for things still in school, or ATELIER for what's part of that plan.

You choose what to make. We choose how.

03The Creator Studio

The workspace that used to be called the pro Studio is now the Creator Studio. Nothing in it has changed: a live queue, a library, every model on a card, an exact creativity slider, seeds and batches. It's still part of Artisan and Atelier.

04Which one are you in?

From now on, paid plans start in the classic Studio. If you'd rather have the Creator Studio, switch it on in your settings; it's saved on your account, so it follows you to every device. You can switch back whenever you like. Your allowance and commercial rights are the same in both. Free accounts use the classic Studio.
