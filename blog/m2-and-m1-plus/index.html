m2 and m1+ are out for preview

01What's new

m1 draws a 64×64 sprite from a sentence. m2 and m1+ do the same job, and they have both been schooled on a much bigger, tidier set of sprites. A few things you'll notice:

  • They listen. Ask for “a knight with a sword” or “a blue potion bottle” and you get a knight with a sword and a blue potion bottle.
  • They know sizes. Say “16x16”, “32 by 32” or “tiny 24px sprite” and the sprite is drawn that chunky. Say nothing and you get the full 64×64.
  • They know backgrounds. Ask for “on a green background” and you get one. Don't, and the sprite is on a see-through background, ready for your game.
  • They can pose. Animals can sit, lie down or sleep, not just stand there: try “a fox sleeping” or “a sitting cat”.

m1+ is the bigger of the two, a larger model that has room for more detail. m2 is a fresh design, a little leaner. Neither replaces m1: it's still there, and still the steady one.

03What preview means

Both models carry a PREVIEW tag. That means they work and you're welcome to use them, but they're new: now and then one will draw something odd, and we'll keep improving them while you use them. If a result is great, or terrible, tell us in the forum. If you need the same look every time, m1 is still the safe pick.

04How to try them

  1. Open the Studio and choose Image. It picks the best sprite model your plan can use.
