01The plans

Since launch, everyone has had the same deal: m1 and 15 generations a day. That stays exactly as it is. Free is still free, and it's still the whole Studio and the whole API.

But some of you are making entire games' worth of sprites, and 15 a day runs out fast. So there are now two more ways to use Mosaic:

Each plan has a mascot, too. The chick is Free, the fox in the work apron is Artisan, and the owl in the beret is Atelier. Wave your cursor at them above: they're watching. The full comparison is on the pricing page.

02The pro Studio

Both plans come with a refreshed Studio, themed to your plan: copper for Artisan, indigo and gold for Atelier. More importantly, it gives you more control over every sprite:

  • Exact creativity. A slider from "follow my words closely" to "surprise me", instead of three presets.
  • Fixed seeds. Same prompt, same seed, same creativity: the same sprite, every time. Roll a new seed, or keep one you like and nudge the prompt around it.
  • Batches. Ask for up to 4 sprites at once on Artisan, 8 on Atelier, each on its own seed, so you can pick the best one. Batches are drawn side by side, so they come back quickly.
  • Big downloads. Save any sprite at 8× or 16×, with crisp pixels and no blur, ready for a store page or a print.
  • v1, on Atelier. Our animated 16:9 scenes, still in preview: 50 clips a day on the Atelier plan.
  • Priority. Paid requests move up the line when it's busy. Free requests still always get their turn: the longer anything waits, the further up it moves, and big batches take turns with everyone else.

Liked the old Studio better? It's still there. Switch back to the classic look any time in your settings: your plan's allowance and rights don't change.

More sprites, more control, and they're yours to sell.

03Selling what you make

Free accounts can use what they make in personal projects, game jams and anywhere for fun. On Artisan and Atelier, it's yours to use commercially: games and apps you sell, client work, ads, merch.

Each creation remembers the plan you were on when you made it, and the Studio shows it right there: commercial use OK. If you ever cancel, everything you made on a paid plan keeps that license for good. As always, our models learn only from art we made ourselves, so there's no scraped artwork behind your sprites. The details are on the license page.
