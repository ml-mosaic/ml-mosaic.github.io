What you can do with what you make

Every sprite and clip you make with Mosaic is yours to use. Your plan decides whether that includes selling things made with it. Here's the short version.

Personal use

Free accounts

  • Use them in personal and hobby projects, game jams, school work and your profile.
  • Share them anywhere. A mention of Mosaic is appreciated, not required.
  • No selling, and no use in products or client work you're paid for.

Commercial use

Artisan and Atelier plans

  • Everything in personal use, plus: use them in games and apps you sell, client work, ads and merch.
  • Edit, recolour and combine them however you like.
  • It's for good: what you made while on the plan stays commercial even if you cancel later.

How to tell which is which

Each of your creations in the Studio shows its license: commercial use OK or personal use. It's recorded when you make it, from the plan you were on at that moment. Through the API, every response carries an X-License header saying the same.

For everyone

  • Our models learn only from art we made ourselves: no scraped artwork. Still, if you ask for someone else's character, logo or brand, using the result is on you, the same as drawing it by hand.
  • Results aren't unique. Someone else can make a similar sprite with a similar prompt, so you can't stop others from using similar results.
  • Don't use Mosaic to make anything illegal, sexual or hateful. We remove that kind of content and may suspend or ban the account.
  • Don't use what you make to build or train a competing image or video generator.
  • Previews can change. Models marked "preview" (like v1) are still learning and may change or pause.
