01When

Dec 6the last day of maintenance
11 daysgive or take

Mosaic's server goes offline on Thursday, November 26, 2026, and stays offline until the end of Sunday, December 6. If we finish early, we'll switch things back on early. If something takes longer, we'll say so here.

02What's offline

Pretty much everything. If it needs our server, it's off.

  • Making images: m1 and every other image model, in the Studio and everywhere else.
  • Making videos: v1 and every other video model.
  • Logging in, signing up and your account settings.
  • The Studio, including your creations, voting and sharing.
  • The API and the developer portal's playground. Requests will fail until we're back.
  • The Workshop app, the forum, gift tickets and claiming them.

What stays up: this website, the blog and the status page. That's where you'll hear from us while we're away.

03Why

Mosaic runs on a small setup, and every so often it needs a proper look under the hood: safety and security checks, updates, tidying up, and fixing the things we've been putting off. Some of that can't be done while everything is running, so we're switching it all off and doing it in one go, instead of taking things down here and there over the coming months.

It's the boring kind of work. It's also what keeps Mosaic, and your account, safe.

04Before it starts

  1. Download anything you need. You won't be able to open the Studio during maintenance, so grab any creations you'll want between November 26 and December 6.
  2. Developers: plan for downtime. If something of yours calls the Mosaic API, expect every request to fail during the maintenance window and make sure it handles that gracefully.
  3. Nothing gets deleted. Your account, your plan and your creations will be right where you left them.

05When we're back

We'll post here and update the status page as soon as everything is back on. Then you can log in and pick up where you left off.

Thanks for bearing with us. See you on the other side.
