Questions

How do I subscribe?

Pick a plan above and subscribe on Patreon. Use the same email as your Mosaic account, and we'll switch your plan on. (A one-click "Link Patreon" button is on its way.)

Is there a free trial?

Yes, for Artisan. The trial runs through Patreon, so you can cancel there before it ends and you won't be charged.

What happens if I cancel?

You go back to Free at the end of the period you paid for. Anything you made on a paid plan keeps its commercial license for good.

How do the daily allowances work?

Sprites and v1 clips are counted separately: Atelier gets 500 sprites and 50 clips a day. Both refill at midnight UTC, across the Studio, profile pictures and the API.

Does priority leave free users waiting?

No. Paid requests move up the line, but every request's place improves the longer it waits, so free ones always get their turn, usually within a minute or two. Big batches take turns with everyone else, and clips never take every worker.

What counts as commercial use?

Games and apps you sell, client work, ads and merch. The details are on the license page.

I liked the old Studio better.

Paid plans come with the pro Studio, but you can switch back to the classic one any time in your settings.
