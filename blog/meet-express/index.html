Meet Express

Some days you just can't wait. Express is the fast lane: flip a switch and your next few sprites and clips skip the line and land in seconds.

01The fast lane

Most of the time Mosaic draws on our shared workers, one thing after another. It's fair, it's steady, and a sprite takes a little while. Express is different: your request goes straight to a big GPU and comes back in a few seconds. Clips that usually take minutes are done before you've finished reading the prompt back.

Same model, same pixels. Just a lot less waiting.

02Who gets it

FreeThe regular line, as always.
Artisan5 Express goes a day.
Atelier15 Express goes a day, for sprites and clips.

A “go” is one sprite or one clip that Express actually drew. When your goes run out, or on the rare days the GPU is resting, everything quietly goes back to the regular line: nothing fails, and nothing extra is used up. Express goes refresh at midnight UTC, along with your allowance.

03How to switch it on

  1. Open the Studio. In the pro workspace, find Express under Settings on the left (in the classic Studio, it's next to Create).
  2. Flip the switch. It tells you how many goes you have left today.
  3. Make things as usual. Express works for the API too, once it's on.

You can also turn it on or off from your settings.
