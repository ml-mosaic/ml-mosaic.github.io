01What's new

Mosaic has a data programme. If you draw pixel art, you can now send us your own sprites to help train the next Mosaic models. A person on our team looks at every set, accepted sprites go into the next training run, and you get rewarded for it.

The short version: send sprites you drew yourself, describe each one, tick the consent boxes, and earn free weeks of Artisan and Atelier as we accept them. Start on the data programme page.

02Why

Here's a rule we've kept since m1: Mosaic only learns from art we made, or art people chose to give us. We don't crawl the web for pictures, and we don't download datasets, not even open-source ones. Every sprite, scene and animation our models have ever seen, we drew.

That rule is the reason you can use what Mosaic makes without wondering whose work it was built on. We're keeping it.

But we're a small team, and there are only so many sleeping foxes we can draw.

The more art a model sees, the more it can do: more poses, more characters, more ideas, more ways to draw a wizard's hat. You've been asking for exactly that. So instead of bending the rule, we're widening the circle: art made by us, and now art given by you, on purpose.

03How it works

  1. Send a set. Sign in, open the data programme page, drop in up to 24 sprites, and describe each one: what it is, what it's doing, anything special. “fox” isn't enough; “a white fox curled up asleep, tail over its nose” is perfect. The descriptions matter as much as the art: they're how the model learns what it's looking at.
  2. We review it. A real person looks at every sprite. Nothing is used until it's accepted, and you'll see which ones were and any note from the reviewer.
  3. It helps train the next models. Accepted sprites go into the next training run, and your page shows which run used them.

04What we're looking for

Yes, please

  • Your own pixel art, from 8 to 256 pixels a side, at actual size (not scaled up). PNG, ideally on a transparent background.
  • Animals doing things: sleeping, sitting, curled up, running, eating, stretching.
  • Characters: wizards, knights, villagers, adventurers, in all sorts of outfits.
  • Things: items, food, plants, furniture, little buildings.
  • Any style. The chunky, cozy look fits Mosaic best, but we'd love to see yours.

Please don't send

  • Anything made or edited with AI, including Mosaic. We only want art made by people.
  • Art you traced, copied, or don't own.
  • Other people's characters, logos or brands, even as fan art.
  • Anything sexual, hateful or illegal.

05What you get

25accepted sprites: a free week of Artisan
+50more (75 in all): a free week of Atelier
+25more, again and again: another week of Artisan

Keep drawing, keep Artisan.

There's no cap. Every 25 accepted sprites after the first 75 is another free week of Artisan, so if you keep contributing, you keep the plan. Weeks start on their own the moment you earn them, line up one after another if you earn several, and are yours to keep. Your page shows how close you are to the next one.

Already on a plan?

Then a milestone for a plan you already have gives you a limit reset instead. A reset refills today's allowance to full, sprites, clips and all, and you choose when: save them in Settings for a big day. (On Artisan, the 75 milestone still gives you a real week of Atelier.)

And if you want, we'll put your name in the release notes of the models your art helped train.

06Your art stays yours

  • You keep the copyright. You give Mosaic permission to use your sprites to train and test its models. That's all.
  • We never sell, publish or show your sprites. Only you and our reviewers can see them.
  • Change your mind any time. Withdraw a set and it's left out of every training run from then on. A model that already learned from it can't un-learn it, but no future one will. Deleting your account withdraws everything.

The full wording is in section 11 of the Terms and Conditions. Questions? Ask us on the forum.

Thanks for helping Mosaic learn. We can't wait to see what you draw.
