HOW IT WORKS

Static in, sprite out.

m1 starts from pure static and sharpens it, bit by bit, into whatever you asked for.

STICKER BOOK

48 wishes, first try each.

We picked the wishes; m1 picked the pixels. One fixed seed, nothing re-rolled. Hover a sticker for its wish, click to take a closer look.

m1 is still in school. It's great at critters, items, plants, buildings and tiles. People, faces and little held things are still wobbly, and it sometimes forgets the hat you asked for. It's practising: this page updates as it learns.
FOR BUILDERS

Put m1 in your game.

  1. Make an account on the sign-in page.
  2. Make an API key in the dev portal (or use your email and password).
  3. POST a wish, get a PNG. Same seed, same sprite. 15 a day.
Open the dev portal 
# a 64x64 sprite, blown up 8x
curl /api/generate \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer MOSAIC_your_key' \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{
    "prompt":     "a little green frog wearing a crown",
    "creativity": 0.5,
    "seed":       1,
    "scale":      8
  }' -o frog.png
