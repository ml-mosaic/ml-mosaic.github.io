01A place to hang out

Until now, the only way to see what other people make with Mosaic was the gallery. Pictures are great, but they can't tell you how someone got that frog to wear that crown. So we built a forum.

  • Show and tell: post your best sprites, your clips, the game you dropped them into.
  • Help: stuck on a prompt, the Studio or the API? Somebody's probably been there.
  • Ideas and feedback: tell us what Mosaic should do next. We read every one.
  • Off-topic: everything else, as long as it's kind.
  • Announcements: news straight from the team.

02Bring your art

When you write a post or a reply, press Attach a creation and pick one of your sprites or clips. It shows up right in the post, crisp pixels and all, so nobody has to squint at a screenshot. (Attaching something shares it to the gallery too.)

The best way to learn a prompt is to see the one that worked.

03Keep it kind

The rules are short: be kind, keep it safe for everyone, and don't post other people's work as your own. Anything that breaks them can be reported with one press, and a real person on the team looks at every report. The forum also turns away anything sexual, the same way the Studio does.

The full rules live in our Terms, and we wrote about how we keep Mosaic safe in Project 27.
