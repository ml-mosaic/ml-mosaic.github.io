DATA PROGRAMME

Teach Mosaic with your art

Mosaic's models only learn from art we made ourselves or art people chose to give us. No scraped artwork, no downloaded datasets, nobody's work taken without asking. If you draw pixel art, you can help the next models get better, and get rewarded for it.

1 · SEND

Upload sprites you drew yourself, each with a few words saying what it is.

2 · REVIEW

A person on our team looks at every set. Nothing is used until it's accepted.

3 · TRAIN

Accepted sprites go into the next training run. Your page shows which run used them.

What we're looking for

Yes, please

  • Your own pixel art: sprites from 8 to 256 pixels a side, at actual size (not scaled up)
  • PNG, ideally on a transparent background
  • Animals in all sorts of poses: sleeping, sitting, running, curled up
  • Characters (wizards, knights, villagers), items, plants, food, furniture
  • Any style. The chunky, cozy look fits Mosaic best

Please don't send

  • Anything made with AI, including Mosaic
  • Art you traced, copied, or don't own
  • Other people's characters, logos or brands (fan art)
  • Anything sexual, hateful or illegal

What you get

A free week of Artisan for every 25 sprites we accept. No cap. Plus, if you want, your name in the release notes of the models your art helped train.

Your art stays yours

  • You keep the copyright. You give Mosaic permission to use your sprites to train and test its models. That's all.
  • We never sell, publish or show your sprites. Only you and our reviewers can see them.
  • Change your mind any time. Withdraw a set and it's left out of every training run from then on. (A model that already learned from it can't un-learn it, but no future one will.) Deleting your account withdraws everything.
