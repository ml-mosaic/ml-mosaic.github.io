Yeah, we know.

Some of what Mosaic makes misses. Here's why, what it means for m1, v1 and everything after them, and what we're doing about it.

01We know

Ask m1 for a fox and you'll usually get a lovely fox. Ask it for something it hasn't seen much of and you might get a shape that's only fox-adjacent. Ask for three things at once and it may pick one. v1's clips can wobble, lose track of what they're showing halfway through, or loop with a little jump.

None of that is news to you, and it isn't news to us either. We'd rather say it out loud than pretend.

Mosaic's models are not as good as the big image generators. They weren't on day one, and the next ones won't be on their day one either.

02Why

Mosaic is small. We don't have a data centre, a research lab or a big budget. Every model we've made was taught from scratch, by us, on a small amount of computer time, some of it free and with weekly limits. A model that the big companies would train in an afternoon on thousands of machines, we train over days and weeks, in turns.

That shapes everything: how big a model can be, how long it gets to learn, how many pictures it gets to learn from, and how often we can start over when something comes out wrong. We could skip all that by building on top of someone else's model. We decided not to. Everything Mosaic draws comes from models we made ourselves, in our own style, and we'd like to keep it that way.

03What it means

  • m1 is good at what it knows: critters, items, plants, little buildings, tiles. Outside of that it guesses, and some guesses miss.
  • v1 is our first try at things that move. It's charming when it works and a bit lost when it doesn't.
  • What's next (s1, c1, the next v-series, and m2 and m1+ when they're back) will be better than what came before. They'll also start out with rough edges of their own. We'll call them a preview while they do.

We'd rather put out a model that's honest about where it stands, and keep improving it, than wait years for one that's perfect.

04What we do about it

  1. Quality over speed. When we have to choose between releasing sooner and releasing better, we wait. Training takes as long as it takes, even if that means across several weeks.
  2. We switch things off when they're not right. You've seen it happen: when m2 and m1+ came out a little too geometric, we turned them off and sent them back to school.
  3. Every model keeps getting better. We keep improving what the models learn from, then teach them again.
  4. We tell you. On this blog: what's out, what's in school, what went wrong, and what we changed.

05What you can do

Vote. The thumbs up and thumbs down on your creations are the most useful thing you can give us. They're how we spotted the geometric problem, and they steer what every model learns next.

Try again. The same prompt again, or with a little more or less creativity, often turns a miss into a hit. Use this prompt on any creation is the fastest way.

Tell us. Post your best misses on the forum. Some of them are genuinely funny, and all of them are useful.

If you're on a paid plan and Mosaic isn't worth it to you right now, that's fair, and you can cancel any time on Patreon. If you stay, thank you. You're a big part of how we keep doing this.

We'll keep getting better. We just won't pretend we're already there.
