Gift someone a week

01A ticket, on you

Sharing is caring, and now it's built in. If you're on Artisan or Atelier, you can gift a friend one week of your plan. They get all of it: the pro Studio, the bigger allowance, Express, v1 if you're on Atelier, and commercial rights on what they make.

The catch (it's a small one): what they make uses your daily allowance. So it's really you lending them your workshop for a week. Very generous. The fox approves.

02How to gift

  1. Hover your picture at the top right of any page. A little menu pops out: Settings and the new Gift a ticket.
  2. Press Gift a ticket. Your mascot shows up to tell you how awesome you are (it means it).
  3. Type your friend's Mosaic username, add a short note if you like, and press Send the ticket.
  4. That's it! You'll see your sent tickets in the same window, and you can take one back if it hasn't been claimed yet.

A few house rules, to keep things fair: someone can only hold one unclaimed ticket at a time, you can have two tickets out at once, an unclaimed ticket expires after 14 days, and gifting unlocks once a free trial turns into a membership.

03Opening your ticket

When someone sends you a ticket, two things happen. You get an email with your ticket on it (the fox or the owl, and the plan's badge) and a Claim your ticket button. And the next time you visit Mosaic, on any page, a little popup with confetti tells you there's mail.

Either way you land on the claim page: an envelope, addressed to you. Click it, it opens, and out comes your ticket in glorious 3D. Grab it and spin it around (we spent far too long on this). After a moment, press Claim my ticket, and your week starts right then.

The week starts when you claim it, not when it was sent. No rush. (But also, why wait?)
